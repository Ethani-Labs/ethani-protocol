📊 PRICING RULES (100% Transparent)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Ratio = Demand / Supply

  Ratio > 1.30  → +15%  (Critical Shortage - Encourage production)
  Ratio > 1.10  → +8%   (Shortage - Incentivize supply increase)
  0.80-1.10     → 0%    (Balanced - Market equilibrium)
  Ratio < 0.80  → -10%  (Surplus - Protect consumers)

Hard Limits: Max +50%, Min -30%
