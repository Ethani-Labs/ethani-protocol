# ETHANI Vision

## Mission
Stabilize food prices and support rural communities through transparent, rule-based logistics and circular energy systems.

## Core Values

### 1. **Transparency**
- Every pricing decision is auditable and explainable
- No black boxes, no hidden algorithms
- All data recorded on-chain
- Farmers and officials can understand how prices are calculated

### 2. **Fairness**
- Protect farmers from price collapse (floor mechanism)
- Protect consumers from price spikes (ceiling mechanism)
- Incentivize production increases during shortages
- Reward efficiency gains

### 3. **Simplicity**
- Rule-based logic over AI/ML
- Deterministic calculations
- Understandable by non-technical stakeholders
- Easy to audit and modify

### 4. **Decentralization**
- Blockchain for permanent records
- No single point of control
- Community governance ready
- Data ownership

## Principles

1. **No AI/ML for Critical Decisions**
   - All decisions are rule-based
   - Humans make policy, not machines
   - Explainability over accuracy

2. **Rule-Based Logic**
   - Supply-demand formulas
   - Threshold-based conditions
   - Simple, auditable math

3. **Blockchain for Transparency**
   - Recording, not trading
   - Data availability
   - Coordination
   - Accountability

4. **Community First**
   - Solutions designed with stakeholders
   - Feedback loops built-in
   - Inclusive decision-making
   - Local optimization
